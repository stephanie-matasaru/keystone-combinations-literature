# Literature: Searching for Impactful Species Combinations in Microbial Communities

Literature collection for my Computer Science bachelor thesis.
Most methods identify keystone species one at a time. My thesis looks at finding the most impactful *combinations* of species in microbial communities, treated as a combinatorial optimization problem, and compares classical and quantum-ready optimization approaches.

## Contents

- `references.bib`: full bibliographic information for all sources
- `notes/`: one note per source (what it is about, what is relevant to my work, what I use it for, limits), named by BibTeX key
- `papers/`: PDFs of open-access sources, grouped by topic. Paywalled sources are linked by DOI in their notes.
