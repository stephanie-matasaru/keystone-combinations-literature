# Addressing ecological challenges from a quantum computing perspective
**Citation:** Clenet2025QuantumEcology · **PDF:** papers/quantum/Clenet2025QuantumEcology.pdf (arXiv preprint)

## What it's about
Review introducing quantum computing basics and possible applications in ecology: statistics (regression, MCMC, PCA), networks (centrality, community detection, trophic flows), and dynamical systems (including Lotka-Volterra models via Carleman linearization). Covers QAOA, quantum annealing with QUBO formulations, and quantum-inspired methods.

## What's relevant for my work
- Names community assembly simulations as a combinatorial optimization problem that could benefit from quantum methods, and mentions keystone species in the context of quantum centrality algorithms -> motivation for a quantum chapter in an ecology-related thesis.
- Lists realistic limits: few/noisy qubits, exponential speedups need fault-tolerant hardware, data-loading bottleneck, warns against overstating quantum capabilities -> supports framing my quantum part as "formulation + benchmark", not "faster".

## Used for
Motivation and limitations of the quantum extension.

## Limits
Preprint; a perspective/review, no experiments.
