# Gut microbiome network ensemble analysis for identifying disease-specific key species in targeted interventions
**Citation:** Luo2025NetEnsa · **PDF:** not included (paywalled), Food Bioscience 68, 106696
**Read:** abstract only

## What it's about
NetEnsa is a multistage approach to identify a compact, disease-specific set of key species in the gut microbiome. Validated on 7,217 metagenomic samples covering 9 disease types from 18 countries, and in humanized mice.

## What's relevant for my work
- Highlights state that a combination of key species enhances gut microbiome diversity and network robustness -> supports targeting species sets.
- Another example of selecting a compact set of species rather than ranking one at a time.

## Used for
Motivation (combinations matter), related work.
