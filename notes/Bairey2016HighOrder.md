# High-order species interactions shape ecosystem diversity
**Citation:** Bairey2016HighOrder · **PDF:** papers/microbiome/Bairey2016HighOrder.pdf (CC BY 4.0)

## What it's about
Theoretical study of random communities with pairwise, three-way and four-way interactions (replicator dynamics). Pairwise interactions destabilize large communities (upper bound on diversity); four-way interactions destabilize small ones (lower bound). Pairwise-dominated communities are sensitive to adding species; four-way-dominated communities are sensitive to removing species.

## What's relevant for my work
- gLV (my model) and the QUBO approximation only include pairwise interactions. This paper shows higher-order interactions can change how communities respond to species removal.

## Used for
Limitations section (pairwise assumption of gLV and QUBO).

## Limits
Theoretical; random unstructured interactions; constant total abundance; the authors note little is known about how strong or common high-order interactions are in nature.
